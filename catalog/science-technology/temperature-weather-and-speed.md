## 工具

### [Celsius to Fahrenheit Calculator — °C to °F](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/celsius-to-fahrenheit-calculator)

Convert Celsius to Fahrenheit or Fahrenheit to Celsius with the NIST scale-and-offset formulas, reference temperatures, examples, and a conversion table.

### [Dew Point Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/dew-point-calculator)

Dew Point Calculator calculates whichever one of air temperature, relative humidity, or dew-point temperature is left blank.

### [Fahrenheit to Centigrade Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/convert-fahrenheit-to-centigrade-calculator)

Convert Fahrenheit to Celsius (centigrade) or Celsius to Fahrenheit with the NIST formulas, direct equality, landmarks table, copy, and CSV export.

### [Feet per Second Calculator — ft/s to mph](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/feet-per-second-calculator)

Convert feet per second to miles per hour or reverse mph to ft/s with exact foot-and-time definitions, formulas, examples, and a speed table.

### [Kelvin to Celsius Calculator — K to °C](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/kelvin-to-celsius-calculator)

Convert kelvin to degrees Celsius or Celsius to kelvin with the exact 273.15 offset, absolute-zero validation, examples, and a landmark table.

### [Wind Chill Factor Calculator — NWS Formula & Chart](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/wind-chill-factor-calculator)

Calculate wind chill with the NWS formula, switch °C, °F, kelvin, and wind-speed units, and review heat loss plus a reference matrix.
