## 工具

### [Aspect Ratio Calculator — Resize & Scale Resolution](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/aspect-ratio-calculator)

Calculate a simplified aspect ratio, compare its decimal value, or scale a resolution by entering any three values. Includes common image and video presets.

### [FPS Calculator — Frames, Time & Non-Drop Timecode](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/frame-per-second-calculator)

Convert frames to elapsed time or HH:MM:SS:FF back to frames. Includes common FPS rates, real duration, frame time, and non-drop labels.

### [IP Subnet Calculator — IPv4, IPv6, CIDR & Netmask](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/subnet-calculator)

Calculate IPv4 and IPv6 networks, ranges, masks, address counts, identifiers, and a bounded IPv4 subnet table from CIDR or netmask input.

### [Nut Calculator — Guitar String & Slot Spacing](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/nut-calculator)

Calculate equal guitar-string edge gaps and every nut-slot center from the bass and treble sides in millimetres or inches.

### [PC Bottleneck Calculator — CPU & GPU Pairing](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/bottleneck-calculator)

Compare desktop CPU and GPU models by resolution, refresh rate, and workload with a transparent pairing gap, pressure report, and verification guide.
