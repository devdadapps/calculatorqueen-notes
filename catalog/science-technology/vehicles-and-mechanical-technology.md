## 工具

### [Fuel Cost Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/fuel-cost-calculator)

Fuel Cost Calculator estimates one-way or round-trip fuel use and cost from distance, fuel economy, and an entered price per U.S. gallon or liter.

### [Gear Ratio Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/gear-ratio-calculator)

Gear Ratio Calculator calculates a single external spur-gear pair's tooth ratio, ideal output speed, and ideal or declared-efficiency torque estimate.

### [Mileage Calculator: MPG, km/L & L/100 km](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/mileage-calculator)

Calculate trip distance and fuel economy from complete-fill odometer readings and fuel added, with MPG, km/L, L/100 km, and optional fuel cost.

### [Tire Dimensions Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/tire-dimensions-calculator)

Tire Dimensions Calculator decodes nominal section width, aspect ratio, and rim diameter into theoretical sidewall height, unloaded diameter, circumference.

### [Wheel Offset Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/wheel-offset-calculator)

Wheel Offset Calculator compares two declared wheel bead-seat widths and ET offsets, showing nominal inner-edge movement, inner-clearance change.
