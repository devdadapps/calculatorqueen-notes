## 工具

### [Alcohol by Volume (ABV) Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/abv-calculator)

Alcohol by Volume (ABV) Calculator estimates fermented-beverage alcohol by volume from original and final gravity entered as specific gravity or degrees Plato.

### [Dilution Calculator — C1V1 = C2V2 with Units](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/dilution-calculator)

Solve C1V1 = C2V2 for stock concentration, stock volume, final concentration, or final total volume with compatible concentration and volume units.

### [Molarity Calculator — Four-Way Solver](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/molarity-calculator)

Calculate molarity, mass concentration, mass fraction, solute mass, final solution volume, or molar mass with basis-aware required inputs and units.

### [Pool Salt Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/pool-salt-calculator)

Pool Salt Calculator handles the pool salt task and opens with Measured current salt: 0; Target salt: 3200. It returns calculated dry salt.
