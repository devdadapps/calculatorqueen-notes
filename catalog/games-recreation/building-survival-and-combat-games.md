# 建造、生存与战斗游戏 / Building, Survival & Combat Games

所属大类：[休闲娱乐 / Games & Recreation](README.md)。本页收录 **6** 个公开工具。

每条说明来自当前页面的结构化内容快照；打开工具后仍应核对页面显示的输入、单位、版本、公式和限制。

## 工具

### [Construction Skill Training Planner](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/construction-calculator-osrs)

Plan an OSRS Construction goal from level or exact XP. Compare verified training methods, planks, actions, material cost and estimated time to your target.

### [Crafting Expected Value Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/the-forge-calculator)

Compare a recipe for The Forge using ore quantities, values, an entered success chance, attempts, and output value, with break-even and expected-value results.

### [Factory Production Line Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/satisfactory-calculator)

Calculate Satisfactory output, input flow, machine count, final clock and ordinary production-building power from one recipe line you enter.

### [Fruit Trade Value Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/blox-fruits-calculator)

Build two Blox Fruits offers from a searchable 59-fruit community snapshot, compare normal or permanent values and check Win, Fair or Loss instantly.

### [RPG Combat Level Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/osrs-combat-calculator)

Calculate Old School RuneScape player combat level from seven skills, with the raw value, dominant style, progress, formula steps, and components.

### [Type Damage Multiplier Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/poke-mon-calculator)

Apply declared STAB, dual-type, critical, and other multipliers to your entered base damage range without loading an outdated Pokémon database.
