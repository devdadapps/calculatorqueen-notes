# 日期与时间计算 / Dates & Time

所属大类：[日期时间 / Date & Time](README.md)。本页收录 **9** 个公开工具。

每条说明来自当前页面的结构化内容快照；打开工具后仍应核对页面显示的输入、单位、版本、公式和限制。

## 工具

### [Age Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/age-calculator)

Age Calculator calculates completed calendar years, then months and days, from a birth date to an explicit as-of date.

### [Date Add Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/date-add-calculator)

Date Add Calculator adds or subtracts either ordered calendar years/months/days or an exact count of Gregorian days from an ISO date.

### [Day Countdown Calculator: Exact Time Remaining](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/day-countdown-calculator)

Calculate the exact signed time between a declared current UTC instant and a target UTC instant, balanced into days, hours, minutes, and seconds.

### [Hours Calculator — Hours Between Times or Dates](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/hours-calculator)

Calculate hours between two clock times or two dated times with 12/24-hour input, visible overnight rules, decimal hours, and duration details.

### [Time and Date Duration Calculator — Compare, Add or Subtract](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/time-and-date-duration-calculator)

Calculate an exact duration between dated times, or add and subtract days, hours, minutes, and seconds from a local date-time.

### [Time Calculator — Add & Subtract H:M:S](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/time-calculator)

Add or subtract two signed hour-minute-second durations, including negative values and hours above 23, with exact H:M:S and unit totals.

### [Time to Decimal Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/time-to-decimal-calculator)

Time to Decimal Calculator converts a non-negative hours/minutes/seconds duration to decimal hours, decimal minutes.

### [Time Zone Calculator: Convert Local Date & Time](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/time-calculator-zone)

Convert a local date and time between supported IANA zones with explicit daylight-saving gap and repeated-time handling for 2007–2030.

### [UTC to EST Calculator: Fixed UTC−5 Conversion](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/utc-to-est-calculator)

Convert a declared UTC date and time to fixed Eastern Standard Time at UTC−5, with the resolved instant, local date, and day shift shown.
