## 工具

### [2026 Army WHtR Calculator: Official .550 Rule](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/army-body-fat-calculator)

Calculate the July 2026 U.S. Army waist-to-height ratio with exact three-decimal truncation, the .550 boundary, and confirmation reminder.

### [ASQ Calculator: Age, Interval & Adjusted Score](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/asq-calculator)

Calculate ASQ age and questionnaire intervals or apply published missing-item arithmetic to a completed licensed ASQ-3 or ASQ:SE-2 form.

### [BMI Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/bmi-calculator)

BMI Calculator calculates metric BMI and applies CDC adult screening categories for people age 20 or older.

### [Body Fat Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/body-fat-calculator)

Body Fat Calculator estimates adult body-fat percentage with the 1984 sex-specific Hodgdon-Beckett circumference equations.

### [Body Type Calculator — Transparent Apparel Proportions](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/body-type-calculator)

Compare upper, waist, and hip measurements with visible balance and waist-definition thresholds for a non-medical apparel proportion shorthand.

### [Bra Size Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/bra-size-calculator)

Bra Size Calculator calculates a starting bra label with either an explicit US entered-band guide or Wacoal Japan's metric table.

### [BSA Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/bsa-calculator)

BSA Calculator estimates body surface area in square meters with the 1987 Mosteller equation from metric or U.S. customary height and weight.

### [Child and Teen BMI Calculator | CDC Extended Percentile](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/body-mass-index-calculator-teenager)

Calculate BMI, CDC BMI-for-age percentile, z-score, percent of P95, and screening band for ages 2-19 using the official 2022 extended method.

### [Child Height and Weight Percentile Calculator | CDC LMS](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/height-and-weight-percentile-calculator)

Calculate CDC height-for-age and weight-for-age percentiles for ages 2-19 with official LMS data, exact age handling, metric or U.S. units, and charts.

### [FFMI Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/ffmi-calculator)

FFMI Calculator calculates fat mass, fat-free mass, raw FFMI, and an explicitly selected height-adjusted FFMI using the original 6.3 coefficient.

### [Height Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/height-calculator)

Height Calculator provides a transparent, population-level adult-height guess from two parent heights using only the Mayo-described midparental method.

### [Waist and Height Ratio Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/waist-and-height-ratio-calculator)

Waist and Height Ratio Calculator calculates waist circumference divided by height in matching metric.
