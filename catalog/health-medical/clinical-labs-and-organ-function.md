# 临床检验与器官功能 / Clinical Labs & Organ Function

所属大类：[健康医学 / Health & Medical](README.md)。本页收录 **8** 个公开工具。

每条说明来自当前页面的结构化内容快照；打开工具后仍应核对页面显示的输入、单位、版本、公式和限制。

## 工具

### [A1C Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/a1c-calculator)

A1C Calculator converts NGSP A1C percent and ADAG estimated average glucose in either direction.

### [Absolute Count Calculator: WBC Differential](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/absolute-count-calculator)

Convert a white-cell differential percentage and total WBC to an absolute count in cells/µL and ×10^9/L with explicit units and safety limits.

### [Absolute Neutrophil Count Calculator (ANC)](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/absolute-neutrophil-count-calculator)

Calculate ANC from WBC, segmented neutrophils, and bands, or add absolute components, with cells/µL and ×10⁹/L results.

### [Corrected Calcium Calculator: Albumin Adjustment](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/corrected-calcium-calculator)

Calculate a simplified albumin-adjusted total calcium estimate in conventional or SI units, with the adjustment and formula steps shown.

### [CrCl Calculator: Original Cockcroft-Gault](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/crcl-calculator)

Estimate adult creatinine clearance with the original Cockcroft-Gault equation, explicit weight choice, kg or lb, mg/dL or µmol/L, and a visible sex factor.

### [DKA Gap Calculator: Serum Anion Gap](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/dka-gap-calculator)

Calculate serum anion gap with or without potassium from same-sample electrolytes, with signed arithmetic and no diagnostic classification.

### [GFR Calculator: 2021 CKD-EPI Adult eGFR](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/gfr-calculator)

Estimate adult eGFR with the race-free 2021 CKD-EPI creatinine equation, explicit mg/dL or µmol/L inputs, full calculation factors, and no CKD staging.

### [LDL Cholesterol Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/ldl-cholesterol-calculator)

LDL Cholesterol Calculator estimates LDL cholesterol from a fasting mg/dL lipid panel with the original Friedewald equation only.
