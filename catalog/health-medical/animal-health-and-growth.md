# 动物健康与成长 / Animal Health & Growth

所属大类：[健康医学 / Health & Medical](README.md)。本页收录 **4** 个公开工具。

每条说明来自当前页面的结构化内容快照；打开工具后仍应核对页面显示的输入、单位、版本、公式和限制。

## 工具

### [Cattle Gestation Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/cattle-gestation-calculator)

Cattle Gestation Calculator adds or subtracts the common 283-day cattle gestation midpoint and shows the cited 279–287 day planning range.

### [Dog Chocolate Toxicity Calculator | Confirmed mg/oz](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/dog-chocolate-toxicity-calculator)

Calculate combined theobromine plus caffeine exposure in mg/kg from confirmed product content, with Merck threshold context and immediate vet guidance.

### [Dog Years Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/calculator-for-dog-years)

Dog Years Calculator returns an approximate human-age equivalent using either the AVMA whole-year medium-dog table or a published Labrador methylome curve.

### [Puppy Weight Table Calculator — Exact Age Checkpoints](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/puppy-weight-calculator)

Estimate an adult planning weight only at the cited 6, 9, 12, 15, or 18-month table marks, with adult-size and planning-range checks.
