# 生活习惯与暴露检测 / Lifestyle & Exposure

所属大类：[健康医学 / Health & Medical](README.md)。本页收录 **4** 个公开工具。

每条说明来自当前页面的结构化内容快照；打开工具后仍应核对页面显示的输入、单位、版本、公式和限制。

## 工具

### [BAC Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/bac-calculator)

BAC Calculator produces an adult educational BAC range from declared ethanol dose and body weight using the 2024 ANSI/ASB fixed volume-of-distribution ranges.

### [EtG Calculator: Standard Drink & Lab Record Audit](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/etg-calculator)

Log U.S. standard-drink equivalents and compare an entered EtG result with its entered lab cutoff—without predicting clearance, detection, or a test outcome.

### [Sleep Calculator: Bedtime & Wake-Up Planner](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/sleep-calculator)

Plan a bedtime from your wake-up time, or a wake-up time from bedtime. Compare complete clock-time windows using a chosen sleep duration or 4–6 cycle estimates.

### [Water Intake Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/water-intake-calculator)

Water Intake Calculator looks up the 2005 National Academies life-stage Adequate Intake for total water and its observed food/beverage components.
