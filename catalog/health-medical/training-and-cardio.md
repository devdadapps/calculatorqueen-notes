# 运动训练与心肺 / Training & Cardio

所属大类：[健康医学 / Health & Medical](README.md)。本页收录 **7** 个公开工具。

每条说明来自当前页面的结构化内容快照；打开工具后仍应核对页面显示的输入、单位、版本、公式和限制。

## 工具

### [Air Force PT Calculator: Current 2026 PFRA Score](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/air-force-pt-calculator)

Calculate a current 2026 USAF PFRA score with all standard events, official age and sex tables, WHtR, medical walk, Tier 2 BFA, and score details.

### [Heart Rate Zone Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/heart-rate-zone-calculator)

Heart Rate Zone Calculator calculates five equal 10-percentage-point planning bands. Review Heart Rate Zone Calculator inputs and display precision.

### [Marathon Split Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/marathon-split-calculator)

Marathon Split Calculator builds an even, faster-second-half. Review Marathon Split Calculator inputs and display precision.

### [Max Heart Rate Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/max-heart-rate-calculator)

Max Heart Rate Calculator estimates population-average maximum heart rate for adults ages 18 through 81 with the Tanaka, Monahan, and Seals (2001) equation.

### [One Rep Max Calculator (1RM): Estimate Your Max](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/1rm-calculator)

Estimate your one rep max (1RM) from weight lifted and 1–10 reps. Compare Epley, Brzycki, Lombardi, and Mayhew methods with kg/lb tables.

### [Pace Calculator — Pace, Time, Distance & Splits](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/pace-calculator)

Calculate running pace, time, or distance; compare kilometre and mile pace, analyze checkpoint splits, convert pace units, and project finish time.

### [VO2 Max Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/vo2-max-calculator)

VO2 Max Calculator estimates relative maximal oxygen uptake from either a Cooper 12-minute distance or the published Rockport one-mile walking regression.
