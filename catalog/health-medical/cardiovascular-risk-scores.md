# 心血管与风险评分 / Cardiovascular Risk Scores

所属大类：[健康医学 / Health & Medical](README.md)。本页收录 **5** 个公开工具。

每条说明来自当前页面的结构化内容快照；打开工具后仍应核对页面显示的输入、单位、版本、公式和限制。

## 工具

### [ASCVD Risk Calculator: 2013 Pooled Cohort Equation](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/ascvd-risk-calculator)

Reconstruct the legacy 2013 ASCVD Pooled Cohort Equation with exact coefficients, strict ranges, US or SI lipids, full trace, and a PREVENT update warning.

### [CHA2DS2-VASc Calculator: Score & Stroke Risk](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/chadsvasc-calculator)

Add CHA2DS2-VASc points for atrial fibrillation, then view two clearly sourced historical Swedish cohort event rates without treatment advice.

### [Mean Arterial Pressure (MAP) Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/map-calculator-bp)

Calculate estimated mean arterial pressure from systolic and diastolic blood pressure, with the weighted formula, pulse pressure, and a clear component graphic.

### [PERC Calculator: Eight PE Rule-Out Criteria](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/perc-calculator)

Audit the eight PERC criteria only after a clinician confirms an adult, low-risk, suspected-PE context, with exact thresholds and safety warnings.

### [Revised Cardiac Risk Index Calculator: Lee RCRI](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/revised-cardiac-risk-index-calculator)

Add the six original Lee 1999 RCRI factors and view the clearly labeled rounded historical major cardiac complication estimate for the resulting score.
