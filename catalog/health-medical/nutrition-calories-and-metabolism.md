# 营养、热量与代谢 / Nutrition, Calories & Metabolism

所属大类：[健康医学 / Health & Medical](README.md)。本页收录 **8** 个公开工具。

每条说明来自当前页面的结构化内容快照；打开工具后仍应核对页面显示的输入、单位、版本、公式和限制。

## 工具

### [Calorie Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/calorie-calculator)

Calorie Calculator applies the 2023 U.S./Canadian Dietary Reference Intake adult maintenance-energy equation for an explicitly selected sex and PAL category.

### [Calorie Deficit Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/calorie-deficit-calculator)

Calorie Deficit Calculator subtracts a user-supplied target intake from a user-supplied estimated TDEE for an adult.

### [Calories Burned Calculator by Activity, MET & Time](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/burn-calories-calculator)

Estimate calories from a 2024 activity or sourced custom MET, body weight, and time; compare gross, above-rest, hourly, and dual-convention results.

### [Macro Calculator: Calories, Percentages & Grams](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/macro-calculator)

Convert daily calories and carbohydrate, protein, and fat percentages into kcal and grams with transparent 4/4/9 math and an optional adult AMDR check.

### [Protein Calculator — Age, Activity & Daily Grams](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/protein-calculator)

Estimate adult daily protein with age, equation sex, height, weight, activity, units, and goal; compare g/kg and 10–35% energy-share results.

### [Steps to Calories Calculator With Measured Step Length](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/steps-to-calories-calculator)

Convert walking steps to distance, time, and estimated calories using your measured distance per step, walking speed, body weight, and an explicit MET.

### [TDEE Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/tdee-calculator)

TDEE Calculator multiplies an explicitly selected adult REE prediction equation by an explicitly supplied long-term physical activity level.

### [Weight Loss Percentage Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/weight-loss-percentage-calculator)

Weight Loss Percentage Calculator handles the weight loss percentage task and opens with Original value: 200; New value: 185.
