# 结构、屋面与室内饰面 / Structures, Roofing & Interior Finishes

所属大类：[建筑家装 / Construction & Home](README.md)。本页收录 **12** 个公开工具。

每条说明来自当前页面的结构化内容快照；打开工具后仍应核对页面显示的输入、单位、版本、公式和限制。

## 工具

### [Board Feet Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/board-feet-calculator)

Board Feet Calculator calculates board feet per piece and for a whole-piece quantity from actual thickness and width in inches and length in feet.

### [Deck Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/deck-calculator)

Deck Calculator estimates straight board courses running along the entered deck length, whole stock boards per course, linear footage.

### [Drywall Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/drywall-calculator)

Drywall Calculator calculates net drywall area and whole sheets after subtracting openings and applying an explicit waste allowance.

### [Drywall Installation Cost Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/drywall-installation-cost-calculator)

Drywall Installation Cost Calculator builds a transparent planning estimate from net drywall surface area, a declared waste allowance.

### [Paint Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/paint-calculator)

Paint Calculator calculates paintable area from room dimensions and explicit opening deductions or accepts a known net area, then applies coats.

### [Paver Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/paver-calculator)

Paver Calculator estimates paver pieces and whole packages for rectangular, square, circular, or triangular paving zones using visible dimensions, paver module.

### [Plywood Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/plywood-calculator)

Plywood Calculator estimates whole plywood packages from rectangular project area, explicit excluded area, actual sheet dimensions, package quantity.

### [Roof Slope Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/roof-slope-calculator)

Roof Slope Calculator converts non-negative rise and positive horizontal run to slope ratio, percent grade, x-in-12 pitch, angle, and rafter length factor.

### [Roofing Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/roofing-calculator)

Roofing Calculator converts a rectangular roof plan and rise-per-12 pitch into sloped area, applies explicit waste.

### [Stair Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/stair-calculator)

Stair Calculator plans one straight stair flight from either a maximum riser or a fixed whole-riser count, and from either one tread depth or a total run.

### [Tile Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/tile-calculator)

Tile Calculator estimates whole tiles and whole boxes from either rectangular project dimensions or a known project area, plus tile dimensions, grout gap.

### [Wallpaper Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/wallpaper-calculator)

Wallpaper Calculator estimates full-height wallpaper drops and whole rolls from total wall width, wall height, opening area, physical roll dimensions.
