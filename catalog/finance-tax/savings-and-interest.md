# 储蓄与利息 / Savings & Interest

所属大类：[金融财税 / Finance & Tax](README.md)。本页收录 **9** 个公开工具。

每条说明来自当前页面的结构化内容快照；打开工具后仍应核对页面显示的输入、单位、版本、公式和限制。

## 工具

### [529 College Savings Calculator — Cost & Monthly Goal](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/529-calculator)

Estimate future college costs, projected 529 savings, coverage, the funding gap, and the monthly contribution needed to fund the goal.

### [Annual Percentage Yield Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/annual-percentage-yield-calculator)

Annual Percentage Yield Calculator handles the annual percentage yield task and opens with Nominal annual interest rate (APR): 5; Compound frequency: 12.

### [Finance Calculator: Solve PV, FV, PMT, Rate or N](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/finance-calculator)

Solve present value, future value, periodic payment, periodic rate, or number of periods with signed cash flows and explicit payment timing.

### [Future Value Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/future-value-calculator)

Future Value Calculator projects the future value of one initial principal alone. Review Future Value Calculator inputs and display precision.

### [How Long Will My Money Last Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/how-long-will-my-money-last-calculator)

Estimate how long a balance lasts with level monthly withdrawals and a constant entered return, plus the withdrawal that fits a target number of months.

### [Interest Percentage Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/interest-percentage-calculator)

Interest Percentage Calculator handles the interest percentage task and opens with Principal: 10000; Time: 3. It returns principal, annual interest rate, time.

### [Saving Money Calculator: Savings, APY & Growth](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/saving-money-calculator)

Plan repeated saving with a starting balance, flexible deposits, APY or a nominal rate, contribution growth, tax scenarios, and an inflation-adjusted view.

### [Savings Bond Calculator: EE & I Inventory Audit](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/savings-bond-calculator)

Total Series EE and I bonds using official Treasury values, audit purchase amount and interest, and flag standard 12-month, penalty, and maturity bands.

### [Simple vs. Compound Interest Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/compute-interest-calculator)

Project an investment with annual and monthly contributions, compare simple and compound interest, and review charts plus annual or monthly schedules.
