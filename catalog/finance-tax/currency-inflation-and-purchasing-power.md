## 工具

### [Inflation Calculator — U.S. CPI, Rate & Basket](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/inflation-calculator)

Convert purchasing power with BLS CPI-U, model a fixed rate, compare two index values, or build a fixed basket with annualized and contribution results.

### [Inflation Rate Calculator — CPI & Basket Index](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/calculator-inflation-rate)

Calculate inflation from two CPI values or a fixed basket, with annualized rate, purchasing-power conversion, charts, contributions, and steps.

### [Peso to USD Calculator: Latest MXN, PHP & ARS Rates](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/peso-usd-calculator)

Choose MXN, PHP, ARS, or another supported currency and convert to USD with dated reference rates, a full rate table, or a custom rate and fees.

### [Pounds to Dollars Calculator: Latest GBP/USD Rate](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/pounds-to-dollars-calculator)

Convert GBP to USD with the latest published reference rate, reciprocal quote, searchable currency table, or your own rate, spread, and fixed fee.

### [Yen to US Dollar Calculator: Latest JPY/USD Rate](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/yen-to-us-dollar-calculator)

Convert JPY to USD with the latest published reference rate, reverse quote, searchable currency table, or a custom rate, spread, and fixed fee.
