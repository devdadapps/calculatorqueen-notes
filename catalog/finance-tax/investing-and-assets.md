# 投资与资产 / Investing & Assets

所属大类：[金融财税 / Finance & Tax](README.md)。本页收录 **6** 个公开工具。

每条说明来自当前页面的结构化内容快照；打开工具后仍应核对页面显示的输入、单位、版本、公式和限制。

## 工具

### [CAGR Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/cagr-calculation-calculator)

CAGR Calculator calculates compound annual growth rate or solves for a missing beginning value, ending value, or elapsed period, with an Excel-ready formula.

### [Dividend Calculator — Income, Yield & Share Count](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/dividend-calculator)

Calculate declared dividend income and current yield from share count, dividend per share, payment frequency, and an entered share price without live quotes.

### [Investment Calculator — Returns, Contributions & Time](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/investment-calculator)

Project an investment balance or solve for the contribution, return, starting amount, or time needed to reach a target, with charts and schedules.

### [Options Profit Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/options-profit-calculator)

Options Profit Calculator calculates expiration-only profit or loss for one long call or long put from explicit strike, premium, contracts, multiplier.

### [Silver Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/silver-calculator)

Silver Calculator converts an entered silver weight to troy ounces, applies an explicit purity.

### [Stock Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/stock-calculator)

Stock Calculator calculates gross and fee-adjusted profit or loss, return on cost, break-even sell price.
