# 预算与负担能力 / Budgeting & Affordability

所属大类：[金融财税 / Finance & Tax](README.md)。本页收录 **4** 个公开工具。

每条说明来自当前页面的结构化内容快照；打开工具后仍应核对页面显示的输入、单位、版本、公式和限制。

## 工具

### [Budget Calculator — Monthly & Annual Plan](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/living-expenses-calculator)

Plan take-home income, living expenses, debt payments, and savings in one clear monthly or annual budget, then review the balance and category breakdown.

### [Cost of Living Calculator: Compare Two Budgets](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/cost-of-living-calculator)

Compare two user-entered monthly budgets by category, calculate the total cost difference, and estimate an equivalent destination salary transparently.

### [Money Calculator: Count Bills and Coins](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/money-calculator)

Count U.S. bills and coins by denomination, or enter custom face values and counts, with exact totals, subtotals, and an itemized breakdown.

### [Prorated Rent Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/prorated-rent-calculator)

Prorated Rent Calculator calculates partial-period rent from an explicitly selected actual-calendar-day, fixed-30-day, or annual-365-day basis.
