# 贷款与信用 / Loans & Credit

所属大类：[金融财税 / Finance & Tax](README.md)。本页收录 **14** 个公开工具。

每条说明来自当前页面的结构化内容快照；打开工具后仍应核对页面显示的输入、单位、版本、公式和限制。

## 工具

### [Amortization Calculator — Payment Schedule & Interest](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/amortization-calculator)

Build a fixed-rate loan schedule from principal, annual rate, and term; review payment, total interest, principal allocation, and ending balance by period.

### [APR Calculator: General Loans & Mortgages](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/apr-calculator)

Estimate APR, payment, interest, fees, and amortization for a general installment loan or mortgage using explicit rate, term, points, and PMI inputs.

### [Auto Loan & Vehicle Affordability Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/affordability-calculator-vehicle)

Estimate an auto loan payment from vehicle price or work backward from a target monthly payment, including term, rate, cash, trade-in, tax, and fees.

### [Boat Loan Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/boat-loan-calculator)

Boat Loan Calculator estimates boat financing, monthly payment, sales tax, upfront cash, total interest.

### [Business Loan Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/business-loan-calculator)

Business Loan Calculator estimates payments, interest, upfront fees, net proceeds, total borrowing cost.

### [Car Loan Price Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/car-loan-price-calculator)

Car Loan Price Calculator handles the car loan price task and opens with Loan term: 60; Fixed annual interest rate: 5. It returns estimated vehicle price.

### [Credit Card Payoff Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/credit-card-payoff-calculator)

Credit Card Payoff Calculator estimates payoff time, interest, total payments. Review Credit Card Payoff Calculator inputs and display precision.

### [Hard Money Loan Calculator: Payments, Balloon & LTV](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/lenders-calculator)

Compare hard-money interest-only and amortizing payments, origination fees, balloon balances, projected property value, owner equity, and refinance LTV.

### [Interest Only Loan Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/interest-only-loan-calculator)

Interest Only Loan Calculator estimates the interest-only payment, later principal-and-interest payment, payment increase, phase interest.

### [Loan-to-Income Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/loan-to-income-calculator)

Loan-to-Income Calculator calculates a loan amount as a multiple of entered gross annual income for one or two applicants.

### [Motorcycle Loan Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/motorcycle-loan-calculator)

Motorcycle Loan Calculator estimates a level monthly motorcycle payment and full amortization from purchase price, down payment, trade-in value, fixed rate.

### [Personal Loan Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/personal-loan-calculator)

Personal Loan Calculator calculates a level monthly payment, scheduled interest, explicitly modeled origination fee, net proceeds.

### [RV Loan Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/rv-loan-calculator)

RV Loan Calculator estimates an RV loan payment, sales tax, scheduled interest, and total purchase cost with cash, rebate, trade-in, negative-equity.

### [Vehicle Refinance Calculator: Payment & Savings](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/vehicle-refinance-calculator)

Compare current auto-loan payments with a proposed refinance APR, term, and fee, including total cost, net savings, and durable break-even.
