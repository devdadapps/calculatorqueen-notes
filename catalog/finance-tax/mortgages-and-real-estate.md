# 房贷与房产 / Mortgages & Real Estate

所属大类：[金融财税 / Finance & Tax](README.md)。本页收录 **9** 个公开工具。

每条说明来自当前页面的结构化内容快照；打开工具后仍应核对页面显示的输入、单位、版本、公式和限制。

## 工具

### [Cap Rate Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/cap-rate-calculator)

Cap Rate Calculator calculates property cap rate from annual NOI or derives NOI from explicit gross operating income and operating expenses.

### [Cash-Out Refinance Calculator: Debt & Cost](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/cash-out-refinance-calculator)

Model a cash-out refinance with entered cash, fees, rate, and term; compare P&I, debt balances, interest cost, and a selected holding horizon.

### [Closing Cost Calculator: Cash to Close Estimate](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/closing-cost-calculator)

Estimate buyer closing costs and signed cash to close from your down payment, entered fees, credits, earnest money, prior payments, and adjustments.

### [HELOC Calculator: Draw & Repayment Payments](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/heloc-loan-calculator)

Estimate an interest-only HELOC draw payment, later amortized payment, payment increase, total interest, and balance schedule under explicit assumptions.

### [Home Loan Affordability Calculator with DTI](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/home-loan-affordability-calculator)

Estimate a home-price budget from DTI limits you enter, debt, down payment, fixed rate, term, property tax, insurance, HOA, and other monthly costs.

### [Home Loan Payment Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/rocket-mortgage-loan-calculator)

Use an independent Rocket Mortgage calculator alternative for fixed principal, interest, taxes, insurance, HOA, PMI, extra payments, and payoff.

### [Home Sale Calculator: Estimate Net Proceeds](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/home-sale-calculator)

Estimate signed home-sale proceeds after entered mortgage and lien payoffs, percentage and fixed selling costs, repairs, preparation, and concessions.

### [Land Loan Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/land-loan-calculator)

Land Loan Calculator estimates a fixed monthly land-loan payment. Review Land Loan Calculator inputs and display precision.

### [Reverse Mortgage Payment & Balance Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/reverse-mortgage-payment-calculator)

Project a reverse-mortgage balance from entered lender terms or solve a level monthly advance to a target, with interest, MIP, fees, and equity charts.
