# 退休、保险与保障 / Retirement, Insurance & Protection

所属大类：[金融财税 / Finance & Tax](README.md)。本页收录 **11** 个公开工具。

每条说明来自当前页面的结构化内容快照；打开工具后仍应核对页面显示的输入、单位、版本、公式和限制。

## 工具

### [2026 RMD Calculator: IRS Table II or III](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/rmd-calculator)

Calculate a 2026 owner RMD from the December 31, 2025 balance with the current IRS Uniform or qualifying-spouse Joint Life table.

### [2026 Social Security Claiming Calculator from PIA](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/social-security-calculator)

Adjust a declared current 2026 PIA for an exact retirement benefit-start month using SSA early, FRA, delayed-credit, and rounding rules.

### [2026 VA Disability Calculator: Combined Rating & Pay](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/disability-calculator-va)

Combine VA disability ratings with whole-person math and estimate 2026 compensation for a supported spouse, parent, and child profile.

### [401(a) Calculator — 2025/2026 Contribution Limits](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/401-a-calculator)

Project a 401(a) balance and screen employee, employer, and related-plan additions against 2025 or 2026 contribution and compensation limits.

### [401(k) Calculator — Balance, Withdrawal & Match](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/401k-calculator)

Project a 401(k) balance, estimate an early-withdrawal net amount, and find the employee contribution range that captures a two-tier employer match.

### [FERS Retirement Calculator: 2026 Basic Annuity](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/fers-retirement-calculator)

Estimate a regular FERS basic annuity from an official high-3 and full service years and months, with the age-62 and 20-year 1.1% branch shown clearly.

### [Inherited IRA RMD Calculator 2026: 10-Year Rule](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/inherited-ira-rmd-calculator)

Calculate a supported 2026 inherited IRA RMD, see whether annual withdrawals and the 10-year rule both apply, and audit the IRS Table I divisor.

### [Military Retirement Calculator: 2026 High-3 and BRS](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/military-retirement-calculator)

Estimate initial gross active-duty retired pay from an official retired pay base, full service years and months, and the High-3 2.5% or BRS 2.0% multiplier.

### [Retirement Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/retirement-calculator)

Retirement Calculator either projects a retirement balance from a fixed contribution or solves the fixed contribution needed for a target balance.

### [Roth IRA Calculator: Declared Growth Comparison](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/roth-ira-calculator)

Project a Roth IRA and simplified taxable account from declared contributions, return, timing, ages, and tax-rate assumptions with annual results.

### [Social Security Payment Calculator from Annual Income](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/social-security-payment-calculator)

Estimate a rough Social Security payment per month and year from your birth date, current annual earnings covered by Social Security, and claiming age.
