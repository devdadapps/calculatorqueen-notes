### [AP World History Score Calculator: 2027 Composite](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/ap-world-score-calculator)

### [APUSH Score Calculator: Raw Points & 1–5 Estimate](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/apush-score-calculator)

### [Cumulative GPA Calculator — Terms, Courses & Target Plan](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/cumulative-gpa-calculator)

### [Final Exam Calculator — Grade Needed & Course Total](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/final-exam-calculator)

### [GPA Calculator — Course, Cumulative & Target GPA](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/gpa-calculator)

Calculate course and cumulative GPA from letter grades or school-issued 0–4.3 points and credits, then plan the GPA needed to reach a target.

### [Grade Calculator — Weighted Grades & Final Goal](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/online-gradebook-calculator)

Calculate course grades with editable assignment rows, numeric or A+–F inputs, weighted percentages or raw points, plus final and remaining grade planning.

### [Macroeconomics Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/macroeconomics-calculator)

### [SAT Reported-Score Superscore Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/sat-score-calculator)
