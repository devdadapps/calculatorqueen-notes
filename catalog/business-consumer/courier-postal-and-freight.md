# 快递、邮政与货运 / Courier, Postal & Freight

所属大类：[商业消费 / Business & Consumer](README.md)。本页收录 **4** 个公开工具。

每条说明来自当前页面的结构化内容快照；打开工具后仍应核对页面显示的输入、单位、版本、公式和限制。

## 工具

### [Freight Class Calculator: 2025 NMFC](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/freight-class-calculator)

Enter packaged dimensions and weight to estimate LTL freight class, density, and volume with the 2025 NMFTA density scale.

### [Parcel Shipping Cost Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/ups-price-shipping-calculator)

Check a UPS shipping quote with a simple package form, then optionally add DIM weight, discounts, surcharges, taxes, and multi-package details.

### [Shipping Quote Audit Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/fedex-shipping-calculator)

Audit a current FedEx quote with package dimensions, actual versus DIM weight, discounts, surcharges, taxes, and all-in or per-package cost.

### [USPS Shipping Calculator — 2026 Retail Postage Estimate](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/usps-shipping-calculator)

Estimate 2026 USPS retail postage for Ground Advantage, Priority Mail, Flat Rate, Express Flat Rate, or Media Mail using official Notice 123 prices.
