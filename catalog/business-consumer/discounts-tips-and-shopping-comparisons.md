## 工具

### [Discount Calculator — Percent or Fixed Amount Off](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/discount-calculator)

Calculate a sale price, savings, original price, or effective discount from any two known values using percentage-off or fixed-amount-off mode.

### [Percent Off Calculator — Reverse & Stack](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/percent-off-calculator)

Calculate original price, percent off, final price, savings, or an optional stacked discount from any two known values.

### [Pizza Size & Value Comparison Calculator — Two Round Orders](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/pizza-calculator)

Compare two round pizza orders by diameter, whole quantity, one-pizza price, nominal top area, order total, and cost per square inch or centimeter.

### [Tip Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/tip-calculator)

Tip Calculator calculates a percentage tip, bill total, and equal per-person shares from a user-selected tip base.
