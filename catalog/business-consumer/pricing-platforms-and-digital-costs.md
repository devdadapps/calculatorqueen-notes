# 定价、平台与数字成本 / Pricing, Platforms & Digital Costs

所属大类：[商业消费 / Business & Consumer](README.md)。本页收录 **7** 个公开工具。

每条说明来自当前页面的结构化内容快照；打开工具后仍应核对页面显示的输入、单位、版本、公式和限制。

## 工具

### [Cloud Resource Cost Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/azure-pricing-calculator)

Reconcile an Azure estimate using dated usage, meter blocks, rates, monthly fees, support, credits, upfront costs, and allocation units you enter.

### [Cloud Usage Cost Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/aws-pricing-calculator)

Build a transparent AWS cost worksheet from dated service usage, block prices, fixed fees, support, credits, upfront costs, and projection months.

### [CPM Calculator — Cost & Impressions](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/cpm-calculator)

Calculate CPM, total campaign cost, or impressions from the other two values with reverse formulas, full steps, and currency-aware results.

### [Margin Calculator — Profit, Stock & FX Margin](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/margin-calculator)

Solve profit margin from any two known values, estimate stock purchase or maintenance equity, and calculate FX margin from a stated quote and leverage.

### [Markup Calculator — Price & Cost](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/markup-calculator)

Calculate selling price from cost and markup, or find direct cost from selling price plus markup, gross margin, or gross profit.

### [Payment Processing Fee Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/paypal-fee-calculator)

Estimate net proceeds or the gross amount to charge using percentage, cross-border, and fixed PayPal fees you enter. No live rate or account lookup.

### [Trade-In Value Calculator](https://calculatorqueen.com/calculators/gamestop-trade-in-calculator)

Estimate GameStop trade-in credit from original price, platform and condition, or audit a current quote with Pro, promotion, cash and deduction inputs.
